Shipping & Returns

Delivery, shipping cost and returns for every Frost & Co. order.

Shipping

Delivery countries and cost. Available destinations and the shipping cost are shown on the secure payment page before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Processing. We start preparing your order as soon as your payment is confirmed. You will receive a payment receipt from Stripe by email.

Tracking. If tracking is available for your delivery, we will email you the details once your order has shipped.

Returns & cancellations

If you are a consumer in the EU, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days after you receive your goods, without giving a reason.

To start a return, email us at frost-co@outlook.com with your order number and the items you are sending back. Please return products unused and in their original condition. Clothing and wearables, including the ArcticHeat™ Thermal Gloves, should be unworn. We refund you after the return arrives.

If something arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, email us and we will make it right.

Questions?

Visit our contact page and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

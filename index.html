About Frost & Co.

One product, done properly.

Frost & Co. is all about winter essentials that work and look good. We start with the ArcticHeat™ Thermal Gloves and keep things simple: one great product, secure checkout and friendly support.

More than a purchase

Warmth should never be a luxury.

We started Frost & Co. around a simple idea: when it gets cold, people deserve to be warm. For most of us, that means pulling on a pair of gloves and stepping outside. It is a small thing. We barely think about it.

For families in Gaza, nothing is small right now. Over the past years, hundreds of thousands of people have been forced from their homes. Many live in tents or damaged buildings, with little shelter, limited clean water, scarce medical care and no heating, while winter nights turn cold and wet. Children feel it first.

We cannot fix everything. But we can refuse to look away, and we can do something small together.

That is why we added a donation option to our checkout. It is not a gimmick and it is not hidden in the fine print. It is one tap, on the secure payment page, every time you order. Whether you buy gloves or not, you can also give directly with the button below.

A small amount seems tiny on its own. But a few euros from one person, multiplied by everyone who reads this page, becomes food, clean water, medical supplies and blankets for people who have lost almost everything. You do not have to solve the whole crisis. You only have to do your part.

1Tap the donate button, or add the donation at checkout.
2Pay securely through Stripe. We never see your card details.
3We pass your donation on to humanitarian aid for people in Gaza.

Give warmth to someone who needs it

Every euro counts. It takes less than a minute, and it can make a real difference for a family facing winter with nothing.
